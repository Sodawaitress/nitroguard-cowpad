03
💡 Value Proposition
Capture nitrogen
before it reaches
the groundwater.
CowPad places replaceable biochar cartridges at the point of urination — trough-side — adsorbing NH₄⁺ passively, triggering an IoT alert when saturated, and returning spent cartridges as slow-release fertiliser. No power. No service visits. No paddock replanting.
FOR FARMERS
Reduce nitrogen leaching without reducing herd size or income
Meet ECan nitrogen limits with evidence, not guesswork
Replace cartridges every 15–20 days, no tools required
FOR ECAN / DAIRYNZ
Farm-level nitrate monitoring at trough scale
Compliance data replacing manual audits
